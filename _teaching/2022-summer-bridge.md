## About the Program

The Math Summer Bridge Program supports rising freshmen from underserved high schools in transitioning to college-level mathematics. Tutored students in college algebra and precalculus.

## Responsibilities

Completed training in evidence-based math pedagogy through the Student Learning Center. Provided tutoring and academic support to help students build confidence and skills before their first semester.

Recommended by the program coordinator for a TA position in the UC Berkeley Mathematics Department based on job performance and student feedback.
