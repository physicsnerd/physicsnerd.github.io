Design, fabrication, and characterization of broadband micro-coil antennas for coherent spin control of nitrogen vacancy centers in diamond. Antennas were designed using E&M simulation (openEMS), fabricated in the Marvell Nanofabrication Lab using photolithography, metal evaporation, liftoff, and wirebonding, and tested experimentally, confirming large improvements in signal-to-noise ratio over previous designs. A high-yield (>95%) manufacturing process was developed for glass and sapphire substrates.
