Presented work on the design, fabrication, and characterization of broadband micro-coil antennas for coherent spin control of nitrogen vacancy centers in diamond. Results demonstrated significant improvements in signal-to-noise ratio over previous antenna designs, with antennas fabricated in the Marvell Nanofabrication Lab on glass and sapphire substrates.
