About
======
Hi! I work as an R&D Engineer at UC Berkeley, maintaining the Donald A. Glaser
Advanced Instructional Labs in the Physics Department. I graduated from Berkeley
in August 2025 with a B.S. in Engineering Physics and a minor in Mathematics.

As an undergraduate, I was selected as a Segrè intern (which is how I got started
in the instructional labs!), did research at LBNL in the Quantum Sensing for Geosciences
Lab under Dr. Zhao Hao, and was a teaching assistant for several classes including 
Physics 7B. I also did research with Professor Alp Sipahigil on color centers in silicon
and Dr. Andreas Zoglauer on machine learning models for COSI.
