About
======
Hi! I work as an R&D Engineer at UC Berkeley, maintaining the [Donald A. Glaser
Advanced Instructional Labs](https://experimentationlab.berkeley.edu/) in the Physics Department. I graduated from Berkeley
in August 2025 with a B.S. in Engineering Physics and a minor in Mathematics.

As an undergraduate, I was selected as one of the 2025 [Segrè interns](https://experimentationlab.berkeley.edu/SegreInternship)
(which is how I got started
in the instructional labs!), did research at LBNL in the Quantum Sensing for Geosciences
Lab on NV centers for magnetometry under [Dr. Zhao Hao](https://profiles.lbl.gov/23942-zhao-hao), and was a teaching assistant for several classes including 
Physics 7B under Professor Catherine Bordel. I also did research with 
[Professor Alp Sipahigil](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Faculty/Homepages/alp.html) in the [Quantum Devices Group](https://quantumdevices.berkeley.edu/)
on color centers in silicon
and [Dr. Andreas Zoglauer](https://andreaszoglauer.com/overview.html) at the Space Sciences Laboratory
on machine learning models for [COSI](https://cosi.ssl.berkeley.edu/).

My CV is available [here](https://physicsnerd.github.io/files/auden_young_cv.pdf).
